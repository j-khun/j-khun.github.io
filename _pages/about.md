<p class="hm-bio">
  I am a climate dynamicist and Postdoctoral Researcher at the
  <b>Research Center for Advanced Science and Technology (RCAST), The University of Tokyo</b>, working with Prof. Yu Kosaka.
  My research focuses on how <b>large-scale atmospheric teleconnections</b>, especially those rooted in the Arctic and high latitudes,
  drive <b>regional extremes</b> such as heatwaves, compound hot-dry events and severe air pollution.
  I received my Ph.D. in Atmospheric Sciences from Kongju National University in 2023.
</p>
